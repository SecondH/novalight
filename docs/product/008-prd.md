## 1. Purpose

This document defines the product requirements, capability boundaries, and future module structure of NovaLight.

This PRD guides:

- product decisions
- architecture evolution
- engineering priorities
- AI development roadmap

Important: Phase 0 is infrastructure only.

No product functionality is implemented during Phase 0.

## 2. Product Scope

NovaLight will become an AI-powered Social Media Management Platform.

The platform will support:

- content planning
- content creation
- publishing workflows
- analytics
- team collaboration
- AI assistance
- automation

## 3. Product Architecture Concept

NovaLight is organized around future product domains:

```text
Identity
  ↓
Organization
  ↓
Brand Management
  ↓
Content Management
  ↓
Publishing
  ↓
Analytics
  ↓
AI Assistant
  ↓
Automation Engine
```

Each domain must evolve independently.

## 4. Core Product Modules

### Module 1 — Identity & Access Management

Purpose: Manage users and secure access.

Future capabilities:

- user registration
- authentication
- profiles
- sessions
- security settings

Priority: High

Dependencies: None

---

### Module 2 — Organization Management

Purpose: Support SaaS multi-user environments.

Future capabilities:

- organizations
- teams
- invitations
- roles
- permissions

Priority: High

Dependencies: Identity

---

### Module 3 — Brand Management

Future capabilities:

- brand profile

- visual identity
- audience definition
- brand guidelines

Priority: High

Dependencies: Organization

---

### Module 4 — Content Management

Purpose: Manage content creation lifecycle.

Future capabilities:

- content drafts
- media assets
- content calendar
- approval workflow

Priority: High

Purpose: Manage social media publishing workflows.

- publishing history

Priority: Medium

### Module 6 — Analytics Platform

Purpose: Transform social media data into insights.

Future capabilities:

- performance dashboards

- reports
- recommendations

Priority: Medium

Dependencies: Publishing

---

### Module 7 — AI Assistant

Purpose: Provide intelligent assistance.

Future capabilities:

- content suggestions
- copy generation
- strategy recommendations

Priority: Medium

Dependencies: AI Platform

---

### Module 8 — Automation Engine

Purpose: Automate repetitive workflows.

Future capabilities:

- scheduled actions
- intelligent workflows
- triggers
- autonomous agents

Priority: Future

## 8. MVP Definition

The MVP should not attempt to build the entire vision.

Recommended MVP scope:

### Included

- User identity
- Organization foundation
- Brand profile
- Content workspace
- Basic AI assistance
- Simple analytics

### Excluded initially

- Full autonomous agents
- Advanced automation
- Enterprise integrations
- Complex AI workflows

## 9. Product Prioritization Framework

Features are evaluated using:

### User Value

Does it create measurable benefit?

### Strategic Importance

Does it support long-term vision?

### Technical Feasibility

Can it be built reliably?

### Operational Cost

Does complexity justify value?

## 10. Product Development Rules

No feature should enter development without:

- clear user problem
- acceptance criteria
- architecture impact review
- security consideration
